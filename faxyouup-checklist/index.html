01

Start with the problem

Nobody wakes up wanting to send a fax. They have an office that will only take one. Say that, and say it plainly.

What I used: I read the live faxyouup.netlify.app page on 4 November 2026 (headline, offer, pricing, FAQ, page titles) and a few German and expat sources, listed at the end. I haven’t seen the design, and I can’t see your Stripe, Telnyx or traffic numbers.
  • Answer

    Stripe is live, the €1 single fax is the main product, and the €24.99 monthly plan is optional. This list is about getting people to it.

  • Answer

    Working answer: “An office I have to deal with only accepts a fax, and I don’t own a fax machine.”

    Who that is, in order of how easy they are to reach:

    • English speakers in Berlin and Germany dealing with the Finanzamt, the Ausländerbehörde, health insurers, landlords and doctors. One expat blog says immigration lawyers advise faxing a German public office because the transmission report proves what pages were sent, which a registered letter doesn’t.
    • Irish and UK people and small firms sending to Germany (your guide pages already cover Ireland and Germany).
    • Germans who fax once a year and don’t want a subscription. Bitkom found 82% of German companies still use fax (2023), so the other end of the line still exists.
  • Answer

    Pain reliever. The headache is two things: finding a way to fax at all, and the fear of a “€1 trial” that quietly turns into a monthly charge. Your page already hits the second one with its comparison table. Lead with the first one in the headline, because that’s what people type into Google.

  • Answer

    My pick:

    1. English-speaking expats in Berlin. You know this crowd, they ask in English, they search in English, and they are already complaining about German paperwork in Facebook groups and Reddit.
    2. People in Ireland and the UK faxing Germany or the EU (solicitors’ clients, anyone with German tax or insurance history).

    German-language search comes next, once the German pages exist (item w2). It has the most volume but the most competition.

  • Answer

    A German comparison article (netzwelt.de) lists AllToFax.de, MyFax, eFax and Fax.to. Quoted prices there: Fax.to about 20 cents plus €3.99 for a one-time send; eFax €0.70–0.75 a page plus monthly costs; MyFax about €0.065 a page plus monthly costs. I don’t know how recent those figures are, so check each site before you quote them anywhere.

    Also seen: SofortFax, Simple-Fax, Fax.Plus (the one the expat blog used). What they teach: the article says to watch for subscription traps and hidden fees. That is the same gap your €1, no-account offer fills.

    Spend 30 minutes on each of the top three: price for a 5-page fax, what the price page hides, and the reviews. Don’t name them in ads.

04

Drop the netlify.app address

A name ending in netlify.app looks temporary, can’t be moved, and is hard to remember. A real domain fixes all three, and it needs doing before you build search presence.

  • Answer

    Try in this order: faxyouup.com (the main one), then faxyouup.de (for the German pages) and faxyouup.ie if the .com is gone. Buy the .com and .de if you can: one runs the site, the other redirects. I can’t see availability from here, so check at your registrar. Some country domains have local requirements, so read the registrar’s note on .de and .ie before you pay.

  • Answer

    I won’t buy domains or enter payment details. Register it in the company’s name (Eriu Technology Limited) so it belongs to the business, and turn on auto-renew and two-step login on the registrar account. Keep the registrar login separate from the site’s.

  • Answer
    1. In Netlify, open the site, then Domain management, then add a custom domain.
    2. Either point the registrar’s name servers to Netlify, or add the records Netlify shows you.
    3. Wait for the free HTTPS certificate. Make the www and bare versions go to one of them.
    4. Set the new address as the primary domain, so the old netlify.app address redirects.

    The site is deployed by dropping in a zip, so every address inside the pages (canonical tag, social tags, sitemap) needs updating in the files before the next drop. Tell me and I’ll prepare it.
